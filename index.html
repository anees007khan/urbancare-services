FAQs

Questions before you book?

Do you provide home and office shifting?

This website presents both as service options. Confirm your team, transport arrangements and coverage before accepting a booking.

Can I request labour by the hour?

You can enquire about hourly labour. The final quotation should specify minimum hours, headcount, overtime, travel and any additional charges.

How is the price calculated?

Pricing may depend on job size, distance, staff count, duration, packing materials, transport, access and special handling. Request a written quote before confirming.
